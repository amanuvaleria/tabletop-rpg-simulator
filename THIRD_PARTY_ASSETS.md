# Third-party assets

## Kenney — Fantasy Town Kit 2.0

- Creator: Kenney (https://kenney.nl)
- Official project: https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit
- Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/kenney_fantasy-town-kit_2.0.zip
- Creator's distribution page on OpenGameArt: https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-town-kit
- License: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal (CC0), https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Original license included at `web/public/models/kenney/License.txt`.
- Files: selected GLB models and their external `Textures/colormap.png` atlas, stored under `web/public/models/kenney/`.
- Adaptations: the app clones model scenes, enables shadows, reduces texture saturation and assembles modular walls and roofs into houses. Files themselves remain unmodified.
- Use: optional medieval houses, market stalls, fountain, cart, sculpted trees, rocks and hedges. The original procedural medieval models remain available.

CC0 permits use, modification and redistribution, including in commercial projects. Attribution is not required, but we acknowledge Kenney here.

No assets or code were taken from Tabletop Simulator or SteamGG files.
